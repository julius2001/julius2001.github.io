I am delighted to share that, together with mezzo-soprano Julika Hing, I reached the final of the "Theatre in Song"
category at the International Vocal Competition 's-Hertogenbosch. For the competition we put together the
programme "Silhouette of Mignon", featuring works by Hugo Wolf, Johannes Brahms, Henriëtte Bosmans and others.
You can watch the video of our performance [here](https://youtu.be/TiGOV4b0yUg?is=gw2Wgwb_6OmQuWpz).
